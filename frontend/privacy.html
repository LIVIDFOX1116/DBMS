FrameCheck privacy policy

FrameCheck is a student course project. This page explains what it stores about people who sign in.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, FrameCheck receives and stores your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile picture link, along with the time you last signed in and the role you have in the app. It asks Google for nothing else, and has no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, show who is signed in, and decide what your account may do. We do not sell this data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Cookies

FrameCheck sets one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is not used for tracking or advertising.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Render and its database on Neon, and both act as service providers. Connections are encrypted. Administrators of this FrameCheck instance can see the list of accounts.

Your choices

You can revoke FrameCheck's access at any time from your Google account connections. To have your account deactivated or removed, write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for FrameCheck.
